# Source Review: Homebrew Organization Reference (2026-08-29)

Source: `Resource/Daggerheart-Homebrew-Kit-v1.0-July-31-2025.pdf`, supplied in the repository.

## Relevant organizational principles

- The opening design section emphasizes balancing narrative focus with dynamic combat, streamlining material, limiting cognitive load, collaboration, flexibility, and asymmetry. For TRPG-PREP this supports separate runtime-facing material for story scenes and combat rather than one overloaded card.
- The adversary section recommends a small set of purposeful features and an ordering that helps a GM scan the block. The transferable lesson is to keep encounter support compact and action-oriented; numeric scaling remains outside this product's contract.
- The campaign-frame section uses a lightweight scaffold: title/concept, tone and themes, an overview, setting distinctions, an inciting situation, GM/player principles, and optional campaign-specific mechanics. The transferable lesson is to distinguish high-level orientation from GM-only operating guidance.
- The location guidance under setting distinctions treats locations as setting pillars, not merely containers for one clue. This supports recurring, returnable, and consequence-bearing location cards in the fantasy board.
- The campaign-map guidance leaves room for the table to add or reinterpret locations. This supports a non-linear runtime location network and argues against persisting a forced route.

## Product translation

For fantasy/adventure, the generator should classify material into:

1. Out-of-combat narrative: factions, motives, social scenes, discoveries, consequences, and recurring places.
2. In-combat scene support: situation, stakes, environment, adversary intent, concise actions/reactions, and aftermath.
3. Travel/transition support: route context, discoveries, complications, and optional detours.

The classification is a preparation aid, not a new rules engine. The board must avoid exposing source-system names or numeric recommendations from the reference.
